Floating Buffer Trainer

A random letter pairs generator for the buffer(s) you choose, in your own lettering scheme.

To practice 3-style, select SPEFFZ (UF) and choose the UF buffer. You can customize your lettering scheme in the Lettering Scheme section.

How to use Floating Buffer Trainer

  1. Choose your buffer(s). Select the buffer or buffers you want to practice.
  2. Set your lettering scheme. In the Lettering Scheme section, enter the letters corresponding to your sticker positions.
  3. Start practicing. Set your timer and start the session to practice random letter pairs for your selected buffer(s).
  4. Practice 3-style. Select SPEFFZ (UF) as your lettering scheme and choose the UF buffer.

Training Tips

1. Practice your letter pairs

Set the timer to 2 seconds and press Start. Whenever a letter pair appears, try to recall the word you associate with that pair in your mind before the next pair appears. Repeat this for every pair throughout the session.

If you struggle to recall a particular pair, stop the session and consult your letter list. Once you have reviewed it, return to practicing. Just one minute of practice a day can make a difference!

2. Learn 3-style commutators

Select the SPEFFZ lettering scheme and set the timer to 6 seconds. Whenever a new letter pair appears, execute the corresponding commutator on your cube, followed by its inverse. Repeat this for every letter pair displayed.

Adjust the timer to suit your pace. As you become more comfortable with the commutators, challenge yourself by gradually reducing the time.

        Lettering scheme

        Start each buffer with a line Name: letters, the letters of the buffer piece. The lines under it, one piece per line, are the pieces that buffer may use. Letters in one line means that those letters belong to the same piece (the program will not generate letter pairs involving letters belonging to one same piece). Separate letters with spaces or commas. The same letters can appear under several buffers. See SPEFFZ(UF) as an example. Use # for comments.
